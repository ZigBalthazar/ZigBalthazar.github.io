I build backend services and tools, mostly in Go and TypeScript, and a fair amount of
that work happens around open protocols. A lot of what I learn doing it never gets
written down — it stays in a terminal history, a scratch file, or my head, and then I
work it out again six months later.

So this is a place to write those things down.

## What goes here

Short notes, each one idea. Not tutorials, not a substitute for documentation — closer
to the notes you'd leave for a colleague who is about to hit the same wall you did.

Some of them will stand alone. Others will be numbered and meant to be read in order,
where one idea genuinely needs the one before it.

## A note on languages

The site is bilingual. Most notes will be in English, some in Persian, and where a note
exists in both you can switch between them with the button in the header — it lands on
the translation rather than sending you back to the home page.
